# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Faith Generation is for adults who want to follow a structured Bible school on mobile. The Eenvoudige modus specifically serves people who have difficulty reading, writing, navigating dense interfaces, or remembering multi-step flows. They should be able to recognize where they are, continue watching, and understand the next action without relying on long copy or unlabeled icons.

## Product Purpose

The app makes a free, structured Bible school accessible through video lessons, supporting content, progress tracking, and module exams. Success means users can return to the app, continue the right lesson with minimal effort, and complete the curriculum without navigation becoming a barrier.

## Brand Personality

Warm, calm, human. Faith-focused without decorative religious clichés. Clear and encouraging without becoming childish or clinical.

## Anti-references

- Empty screens presented as simplicity
- Icon-only navigation that requires users to remember meanings
- Sterile utility UI with no visual anchor or sense of welcome
- Dense cards, long paragraphs, small touch targets, and competing actions
- Childlike accessibility design that talks down to adults
- Decorative motion or color that distracts from watching the lesson

## Design Principles

1. Put the next video first.
2. Recognition beats recall: pair familiar icons with short labels.
3. One dominant action per screen.
4. Reduce reading without removing orientation or reassurance.
5. Preserve adult dignity through calm, polished, familiar interaction patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Design for low literacy and reduced digital confidence.
- Use short Dutch labels, large touch targets, strong hierarchy, and predictable navigation.
- Never depend on color or icons alone to communicate meaning.
- Support system accessibility settings and avoid unnecessary motion.
- Keep the standard curriculum and unlock rules intact while reducing navigation effort.
